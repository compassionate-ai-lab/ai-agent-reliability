# Agent incident record

Record the mission and system version, detection, user-visible symptom, confirmed consequences, containment, pending effects, authoritative receipts, affected records and people, first invalid state, propagation, missed barriers, and contributing causes.

Separate confirmed facts, hypotheses, and unresolved ordering. Identify an owner and evidence for consequence containment, service restoration, and affected-person remediation. Retain the regression case and the legitimate-neighbor tests. Do not close the incident while required remediation remains unowned.
